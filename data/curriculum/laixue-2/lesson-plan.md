## Lesson 1 — The Weather Is Nice Today
- Level: L1
- Topic: Weather
- Objectives: List the four seasons; Describe the weather of the four seasons; Compare the weather of different places; Discuss the weather forecast
- Scenario: park-weather — Run into Mingwen in the park and chat about today's weather, the four seasons, and tomorrow's forecast
- NPC: mingwen — 明文 (Mingwen), a kind, friendly graduate student who runs in the park
- Journal prompts:
  1. Describe the weather in each of the four seasons where you live.
  2. Compare the weather of two places you know (for example your home and Taiwan).
  3. Say what you can and cannot do when it rains, and what you hope the weather will be tomorrow.

## Lesson 2 — I Would Like to Buy Gifts for My Parents
- Level: L1
- Topic: Shopping (I)
- Objectives: Talk about what gifts to buy for family; List the advantages of shopping online; Compare the differences of shopping online versus in- person
- Scenario: gift-shopping — Lisa asks what gifts you want to buy for your family and whether you shop online
- NPC: lisa — 麗莎 (Lisa), Gloria's sweet, warm-hearted coworker
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write who you want to buy gifts for and what you will buy for each person.
  2. Explain why you like (or do not like) shopping online, using 因為……所以…….
  3. Describe a shop or a gift that is both cheap and good (又……又……).

## Lesson 3 — The Braised Fish Is Very Delicious
- Level: L1
- Topic: Food and Taste
- Objectives: List the names of common foods; Describe the taste of foods and ways of cooking; Express liking for foods; Order Chinese dishes in a restaurant
- Scenario: restaurant-order — Order a meal from the waiter: fish, vegetables, soup and drinks
- NPC: waiter — 服務員 (waiter), a polite, quick waiter in a Chinese restaurant
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write what you like to eat and what you do not eat at all (什麼都不……).
  2. Describe a meal you had in a restaurant: what you ordered and how it tasted.
  3. Make a suggestion to a friend about where to eat or what to order (……吧).

## Lesson 4 — I Would Like a Cup of Hot Coﬀee
- Level: L1
- Topic: Ordering Beverages
- Objectives: Order beverages at a coffee shop; List the common beverages and foods at a coffee shop; Express liking for beverages and foods
- Scenario: coffee-shop — Order a drink and a sandwich at a coffee shop, hot or iced, and ask for the same as a friend
- NPC: barista — 店員 (shop assistant), a friendly young coffee shop clerk
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write what you usually order at a coffee shop and whether you prefer hot or iced.
  2. Say what you will do if the weather is hot or cold (如果……就……).
  3. Compare two things that are the same (跟……一樣), for example two drinks or two friends.

## Lesson 5 — What Do You Think about This Coat?
- Level: L1
- Topic: Shopping (II)
- Objectives: Identify different articles of clothing; Describe the color, size and price of clothing; Express likes/dislikes and comment on clothing; Identify different methods of payment during shopping
- Scenario: clothes-shop — Ask a clerk about a coat or skirt, its price and how to pay
- NPC: clerk — 店員 (shop assistant), a polite clothing store clerk
- Journal prompts:
  1. Describe something you bought recently and how much it cost.
  2. Write about a change in your life using 了 (for example 我不喝咖啡了).
  3. Use 雖然……可是…… to say something is nice but has a problem.

## Lesson 6 — We Are Moving Next Month
- Level: L1
- Topic: Moving
- Objectives: Talk about directions and locations; Describe distance between two places (e.g., near or far); List the facilities nearby
- Scenario: moving-house — Jose asks about your new home: how far it is, how long the drive is and what you will do next month
- NPC: jose — 何希 (Jose), Gloria's husband, who loves cooking and swimming
- Journal prompts:
  1. Describe where you live now and how far it is from your school or office.
  2. Write how long it takes to get from your home to a place you often go.
  3. Describe the kind of place you want to live in (住的地方……).

## Lesson 7 — The Supermarket Is Next to the Swimming Pool
- Level: L1
- Topic: Neighborhood and Surroundings
- Objectives: Identify locations and positions; Name common rooms of a house; Talk about the neighborhood and nearby facilities of a residence
- Scenario: find-supermarket — Jiawen needs to find the supermarket and the swimming pool; give directions with positional words
- NPC: jiawen — 家文 (Jiawen), a lively, cheerful high-school student visiting the city
- Journal prompts:
  1. Describe where things are near your home (超級市場在……).
  2. Write how many people or things there are in a place, using 幾.
  3. Describe your neighborhood: what is in front of, behind, left and right of your home.

## Lesson 8 — A Typical Day for Gloria
- Level: L1
- Topic: Daily Life
- Objectives: Describe the daily routine; Talk about sequences of events; Talk about habitual activities
- Scenario: daily-routine — Kevin asks about your typical day: when you get up, what you eat and what you do first and then
- NPC: kevin — 凱文 (Kevin), Gloria's easy-going coworker
- Journal prompts:
  1. Describe your typical day with 先……，然後…….
  2. Write what you did yesterday using 了.
  3. Write what you like to do at home in the evening, using a repeated verb (聽聽音樂).

## Lesson 9 — I Caught a Cold
- Level: L1
- Topic: Getting Sick
- Objectives: Describe the symptoms of a cold; Understand instruction from a doctor; Talk about how to stay healthy
- Scenario: doctor-visit — Tell the doctor you caught a cold, how long you have been sick and what you did
- NPC: doctor — 醫生 (doctor), a calm, caring doctor
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write what you should do more or less of when you are sick (多……, 少……).
  2. Describe a day you did not do something (沒……) because you were not well.
  3. Write about something that happened again (又……了).

## Lesson 10 — Gloria Wants to Visit Taiwan
- Level: L1
- Topic: Future Plans
- Objectives: Describe on-going activities; Talk about future plans; Tell others your experiences
- Scenario: plan-taiwan-trip — Mingwen asks what you are doing now, when you will go to Taiwan and where you have been
- NPC: mingwen — 明文 (Mingwen), a kind, friendly graduate student who helps Gloria practise
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write what you are doing right now, using 在.
  2. Write what you will do as soon as something happens (一……就……) and what is about to happen (快要……了).
  3. Write about places you have been and places you have never been (過 / 沒……過).
