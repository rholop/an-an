## Lesson 1 — I Want to Go to Taiwan
- Level: L2
- Topic: Work
- Objectives: Explain the reason for a job change.; Explain the circumstances for a job change.; Describe your future [life] plans.
- Scenario: job-change-news — Mingwen hears Gloria's news about the job in Taiwan: why, how it came about, her worries and her plans
- NPC: mingwen — 明文 (Mingwen), a kind, friendly graduate student who helps Gloria practise
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about a job or life change and the reason for it (的話, 除了……還 / 也……).
  2. Write a paragraph about what you hope to do in a new place besides working (除了……還 / 也……).
  3. Write a paragraph about what you would ask a friend to do for you (請你把……V一V), and what you will do if something happens (……的話).

## Lesson 2 — Welcome to Taiwan
- Level: L2
- Topic: At the Airport
- Objectives: Talk about how an air flight went.; Describe locations and facilities at an airport.; Grasp the traffic situation near an airport.; Express gratitude and concern.
- Scenario: airport-pickup — Jiawen meets you at Taoyuan airport: the flight, luggage, being tired and hungry, directions and transport
- NPC: jiawen — 家文 (Jiawen), a lively, cheerful Taiwanese high-school student
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about a long trip: how tired, hungry or busy you were (……得不得了, 一點都不……).
  2. Write the steps of how you get from an airport to your hotel or home (先……再……, 往……).
  3. Write a paragraph about the traffic and transport near an airport you know (往, 先……再……, ……得不得了).

## Lesson 3 — I Have Rented a Pretty Nice Apartment
- Level: L2
- Topic: Renting a place to live
- Objectives: State your residential needs.; Describe the surroundings of your residence.; State the circumstances of renting a place to live.
- Scenario: renting-apartment — The landlord shows you an apartment: surroundings, rent, furniture and your needs
- NPC: landlord — 房東 (landlord), a friendly, businesslike landlord who knows the neighborhood
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about the home you want to rent and what you need (另外, 算是).
  2. Write a paragraph comparing two places to live: price, noise, distance (算是 / 不算, 再加上).
  3. Write a paragraph about what you will do after you move, step by step (等……再……, 另外).

## Lesson 4 — The MRT is Fast and Convenient
- Level: L2
- Topic: Transportation
- Objectives: Describe means of transportation used in daily life.; Explain the pros and cons of different means of transportation.; Describe what it is like to take [various] means of transport.
- Scenario: commute-choices — Kevin asks how you get to work in Taipei: MRT, bus, taxi or bike, tickets and what is convenient or dangerous
- NPC: kevin — 凱文 (Kevin), Gloria's easy-going coworker who knows every transport option
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about how you go to work or school and why you chose it (才, 難怪).
  2. Write a paragraph about the good and bad points of two means of transport (難怪, 才).
  3. Write a paragraph about something you can freely choose, using a question word twice (想……什麼就……什麼).

## Lesson 5 — The Night Market is Really Bustling
- Level: L2
- Topic: Taiwan’s night markets
- Objectives: Express how much you like something to which you look forward.; Explain why night markets are popular.; Describe the taste and characteristics of foods.
- Scenario: night-market-tour — Lisa takes you to a night market: what to eat, how it smells and tastes, and why it is popular
- NPC: lisa — 麗莎 (Lisa), Gloria's sweet, warm-hearted coworker who knows the night markets
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about a food you tried or want to try at a night market (早就……了, 會……的).
  2. Write a paragraph about why night markets are popular and what they mean to you or to tourists (對……來說, 不管……都……).
  3. Describe the taste and look of a food with doubled words such as 熱熱的、酥酥脆脆的 (VsVs的).

## Lesson 6 — The Features of Taipei
- Level: L3
- Topic: Cityscape
- Objectives: Describe city scenes.; Compare differences among cities.; Talk about city life.
- Scenario: taipei-features — Kevin asks what you think of Taipei: buildings, history, tourists, food queues and how it compares with other cities
- NPC: kevin — 凱文 (Kevin), Gloria's easy-going coworker who knows the city well
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about how long you have been somewhere and what you are doing now (……了……了, V著).
  2. Write a paragraph about what a city is connected to: history, religion, weather (跟……有關係).
  3. Write a paragraph comparing two cities (跟……比起來) and say how long something took you (……才……).

## Lesson 7 — Favorite Leisure Activities of Taiwanese People
- Level: L3
- Topic: Leisure Activities
- Objectives: Expound on leisure activities that people like.; Explain why leisure activities are popular.; Describe your feelings while taking part in leisure activities.
- Scenario: leisure-talk — Mingwen chats about favourite leisure: ball games, being a fan, shouting for the team and a night at KTV
- NPC: mingwen — 明文 (Mingwen), a kind, friendly graduate student who loves baseball
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about something you never did before (從來沒……) and how your interest changed (本來……，後來……).
  2. Write a paragraph about what makes a leisure activity enjoyable if one condition is met (只要……就……).
  3. Write a paragraph about how people differ in character, habits or work (在……上).

## Lesson 8 — Attending a Wedding Ceremony
- Level: L3
- Topic: Wedding Ceremonies
- Objectives: Elucidate Chinese marriage customs.; Describe a wedding ceremony.; Learn about Chinese gift-giving taboos.
- Scenario: wedding-invitation — Mingwen invites you to a friend's wedding: the invitation, red envelope customs, clothes, the ceremony and blessings
- NPC: mingwen — 明文 (Mingwen), a kind, friendly graduate student who knows the customs
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about a custom or celebration you know and when it starts (從……起 / 開始, 尤其).
  2. Write a paragraph about what people care about at a wedding, using 連……都 / 也 for emphasis.
  3. Write a paragraph about something you can or cannot recognise or work out (V得 / 不出來).

## Lesson 9 — Taiwan’s Indigenous Peoples
- Level: L3
- Topic: Indigenous Peoples
- Objectives: Give an overview of Taiwan’s Indigenous Peoples.; Describe some cultural characteristics of Taiwan’s Indigenous Peoples.; Talk about cultural events, objects or practices you find interesting.
- Scenario: island-trip — Jiawen plans a trip around Taiwan with you: eastern coast, indigenous festivals, handicrafts and what you must see
- NPC: jiawen — 家文 (Jiawen), a lively, cheerful Taiwanese student who loves travelling
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about the places you want to visit and list them (A、B、C 等).
  2. Write a paragraph about how two groups or people do things differently (各V各的).
  3. Write a paragraph about something you absolutely must do on a trip (非……不可).

## Lesson 10 — My Taiwan Experience
- Level: L3
- Topic: Experience with living in another country
- Objectives: Talk about living in a foreign country.; Explain an unforgettable life experience.; Describe how you feel about something.
- Scenario: taiwan-experience — Mingwen asks about your life in Taiwan as it nears its end: feelings, souvenirs, what you learned and what is typical
- NPC: mingwen — 明文 (Mingwen), a kind, friendly graduate student and close friend
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about how living abroad brought you something (給……帶來……).
  2. Write a paragraph about packing and souvenirs: what fits and what does not (V得 / 不下).
  3. Write a paragraph about how things usually are in a place you know (一般來說).
