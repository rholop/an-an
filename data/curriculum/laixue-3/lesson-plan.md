## Lesson 1 — Welcoming a New Classmate
- Level: L2
- Topic: Introducing oneself
- Objectives: Ask others about people, things and events.; Describe how you lived during a certain period of time.; Introduce your name and your job.; Describe your interest (hobby).
- Scenario: first-day-class — Introduce yourself to Teacher Lin and the class on the first day: name, job, how long you studied, and your hobby
- NPC: teacher-lin — 林老師 (Teacher Lin), a warm, patient Mandarin teacher
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a short paragraph introducing yourself: your name, your job, and which day or week was your first at work (use 第 and 多).
  2. Write a paragraph about your hobby: what you did before (以前) and what you hope to do later (以後).
  3. Write a paragraph saying where someone or something is exactly (就在 / 就是) and how long you have studied Mandarin (……多).

## Lesson 2 — The Seaside Scenery Is Beautiful
- Level: L2
- Topic: Vacation
- Objectives: Describe the scenery and features of your vacation.; Describe the means of transportation used.; Describe what you did at the beach.; Describe how your vacation felt.
- Scenario: beach-vacation — Dushyant asks about your beach vacation: how you got there, what you did, the scenery and how it felt
- NPC: dushyant — 杜翔 (Dushyant), a hard-working, friendly engineer who needs a holiday
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about a vacation: where you went and how you got there (是……的, 怎麼).
  2. Describe the scenery and how things felt, while doing two things at once (……起來, 一邊……一邊……).
  3. Write a paragraph about what you did at the beach and how the holiday made you feel (一邊……一邊……, ……起來).

## Lesson 3 — Thanksgiving Holiday
- Level: L2
- Topic: Holidays
- Objectives: Ask others what they think.; Explain your plans and arrangements for spending a holiday.; Explain your feelings about spending a holiday.; Enumerate Thanksgiving dishes.
- Scenario: thanksgiving-plans — Gloria plans a Thanksgiving dinner: dates, dishes, who to invite and what to prepare earlier or later
- NPC: gloria — 高莉亞 (Gloria), an outgoing coworker who loves hosting friends
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write about when you are free and busy (從……到……) and what you will do earlier or later (早一點 / 晚一點).
  2. Write a paragraph about the dishes for a holiday meal and whether they should be bigger, sweeter or cheaper (……一點).
  3. Write a paragraph about how you would spend Thanksgiving or another holiday and who you would invite (從……到……, ……一點).

## Lesson 4 — The Company Sent Me to Work Here
- Level: L2
- Topic: Work
- Objectives: Describe your current work situation.; Describe the characteristics of your company.; Describe what you think about your future.
- Scenario: new-branch-office — Mingwen asks about your new job at the branch office: why you came, the company, your experience and your future
- NPC: mingwen — 明文 (Mingwen), a kind, friendly graduate student who helps Gloria practise
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about your work situation: what your company sent you to do and how it is going so far (還, 到).
  2. Write a paragraph about what you are interested in and what experience you have (對……有……).
  3. Write a paragraph about how people at your company treat you and what you think about your future (對, 還).

## Lesson 5 — Oolong Tea Is Very Aromatic
- Level: L2
- Topic: Drinking tea
- Objectives: Learn about Chinese tea-drinking customs.; Enumerate basic facts about tea.; Describe the taste of tea.
- Scenario: tea-shop — Buy oolong tea from the tea seller: smell it, taste it, compare teas, and learn how to steep it
- NPC: tea-seller — 老闆 (tea seller), a friendly tea shop owner who loves to explain tea
- Journal prompts:
  1. Describe a tea or drink you like: its smell, taste and how it differs from another (沒有……那麼……, 聞聞看).
  2. Write a paragraph about tea or coffee habits in your family or country, using verbs like 喝了、泡過 (verb–object verbs).
  3. Write a paragraph comparing two drinks or foods and suggest trying one (……看, 沒有……那麼……).

## Lesson 6 — My Colleagues Are Very Good to Me
- Level: L2
- Topic: Getting along with others
- Objectives: Describe your work situation.; Describe the personality of another person.; Describe how you get along with colleagues.; Express solicitude for others.
- Scenario: new-colleagues — Joann asks how you are getting along at the new company: colleagues, work pressure, and what you understand
- NPC: joann — 張家安 (Joann), a thoughtful trade company manager who cares about colleagues
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about your work: what you have finished and what you did not finish (V完, 沒……完).
  2. Write a paragraph about your colleagues: some are lively, some are hard-working (有的……有的……).
  3. Write a paragraph about something you can or cannot understand, and how you take care of yourself (V得 / V不 + complement, 好好地).

## Lesson 7 — I Am Going to Europe on Business
- Level: L2
- Topic: Taking a business trip
- Objectives: Describe the nature and purpose of a business trip.; Describe the results of holding discussions with others.; Describe the surroundings and transportation of a certain locale.
- Scenario: europe-business-trip — Dushyant asks about your business trip to Europe: why you go, what you discussed, transport and culture
- NPC: dushyant — 杜翔 (Dushyant), a hard-working, friendly engineer who travels for work
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about a business trip: what you found, bought or finished arranging (V到, V好).
  2. Write a paragraph about how a city or your life is changing (越來越……).
  3. Write a paragraph about something that happened only just recently or only a little (才).

## Lesson 8 — I Made a Mistake
- Level: L2
- Topic: Introspection
- Objectives: Talk about a mistake you made while at work.; Explain why the mistake happened at work.; Express your feelings.
- Scenario: mistake-at-work — The manager asks about the mistake in the contract: what happened, how you feel and how you will fix it
- NPC: manager — 經理 (manager), a fair but strict manager who values careful work
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about a mistake you made at work or school and what you did wrong (V錯).
  2. Write a paragraph about something that happened to you and how it made you feel (被, 讓).
  3. Write a paragraph about the tools you use to do your work, such as the computer or a language (用).

## Lesson 9 — Weekend Activities
- Level: L2
- Topic: Arranging how you spend your life
- Objectives: Describe how you usually spend your weekends.; Describe the housework you often do on weekends.; Describe how to arrange weekend activities.
- Scenario: weekend-plans — Jose asks how you arrange the weekend: housework, moving things in and out, and an outing
- NPC: jose — 何希 (Jose), Gloria's husband, who loves cooking, swimming and grilling
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about the housework you do at the weekend and who carries things up, down, in or out (……上來 / 下去 / 出去).
  2. Write a paragraph about what you do with the things in your home (把……拿出去 / 搬進來).
  3. Write a paragraph about weekend activities, using a topic first (這些……我都……).

## Lesson 10 — My Mandarin Is Improving
- Level: L2
- Topic: Language learning
- Objectives: Describe the levels of foreign language learning.; Review learning problems.; Describe your state of progress.
- Scenario: progress-chat — Teacher Lin asks how your Chinese is improving: exam, listening, tones, characters and study habits
- NPC: teacher-lin — 林老師 (Teacher Lin), a warm, patient Mandarin teacher
- Journal prompts:
  1. Write a paragraph about your progress in Chinese: what is better now (更, 不但……而且……).
  2. Write a paragraph about what you handed in, gave back or put somewhere (把……給 / 到 / 在).
  3. Write a paragraph about something you do not know yet and what you will do to find out (不知道, 不但……而且……).
